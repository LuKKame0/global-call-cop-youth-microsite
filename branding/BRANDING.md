# The Global Call Brand Guidelines

Art Direction 2026

The Global Call is a global platform that connects young people, communities, and allies to drive policy and actions that generate real impact.

Our call is global. Our action is local.

## Brand Identity

### Logo

The logo represents an open conversation and a global space where voices come together to drive solutions.

Use the logo with strong contrast and enough breathing room so it remains clear at every size.

### Clear Space

Always keep a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter `G` in the logo.

Do not place text, icons, borders, or other visual elements inside this safe area.

Use color as a signal, not decoration. The general visual environment should stay clean, mostly black, white, and grayscale. Brand colors should highlight meaning, category, motion, calls to action, and navigation.

## Typography

The typography should feel clean, modern, and highly legible for digital environments.

### Headlines

Use `Inter Bold` or `Inter ExtraBold` for titles, headers, and key messages.

Headlines should be direct, confident, and easy to scan.

### Body Text

Use `Inter Regular` or `Inter Medium` for long-form text, descriptions, captions, and paragraphs.

Body copy should stay readable, concise, and well-spaced.

## Content Style

The visual content style should be dynamic, authentic, and action-driven.

Recommended content formats:

- Country updates
- Impact metrics
- Youth engagement stories
- Field content
- Media spotlights
- Action calls

Content should feel real and close to the people involved. Avoid generic stock-style communication when possible.

## Tone Of Voice

The tone should be:

- Direct: communicate clearly and get to the point.
- Actionable: inspire people to take action.
- Inclusive: make all voices matter.
- Global and local: think globally and act locally.

Use language that is optimistic, practical, and grounded in real-world action.

## Photography Style

Photography should use real images that tell stories of impact.

Prioritize:

- Collaboration
- Diversity
- Proactiveness
- Community

Images should feel warm, human, energetic, and credible. They should show people participating, creating, organizing, learning, and leading.

## Image Generation Guide

When generating or selecting images, follow these principles:

1. Define the impression you want to share: excellence, collaboration, empathy, opportunity, or action.
2. Think about the values of The Global Call: inclusivity, joy in action, collaboration, proactivity, dynamism, and trust.
3. Use those values to propose scenes that represent the brand authentically.

## Application Examples

The identity can be applied across:

- Social media posts
- Mobile screens
- Tote bags
- Bottles
- Banners
- Presentation assets
- Reports
- Campaign materials

Keep applications high contrast, simple, and energetic. Use the logo clearly and use color blocks or accent icons to identify the content category.

## Brand Message System

Core message:

> A global call. Many voices. A better future.

Supporting messages:

- Connect: we connect people and communities around the world.
- Inspire: we spark collective actions that drive real change.
- Amplify: we amplify young voices and diverse perspectives for global justice.
- Transform: we transform policies and systems for a sustainable future.

## Practical Usage Notes

- Use grayscale as the primary visual base.
- Use blue, green, pink, and orange as intentional accents.
- Keep layouts spacious and easy to read.
- Preserve logo clear space.
- Avoid overcrowding the design with too many colors at once.
- Pair strong headlines with authentic human imagery.
- Use icons and category colors consistently across campaigns.
